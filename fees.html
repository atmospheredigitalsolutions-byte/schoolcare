How your fees are built

Charges fall into three groups, so there are no surprises on your invoice.

Once, on admission

One-time charges

  • Registration / acceptance fee, which secures your child's place
  • Admission processing and ID card
  • Welcome pack, where provided
Every term

Termly charges

  • Tuition for the class your child is placed in
  • Learning materials and continuous assessment
  • Any termly levies listed in the schedule above
As needed

Billed separately

  • School uniform and sportswear
  • Textbooks and exercise books
  • School transport and lunch, where used
  • Excursions, events and optional extra lessons

Payment policy

The standard terms that apply to every family. Please read them before making a payment.

Where to pay

Pay into the school's official account only. Use your child's full name and class as the payment reference, and keep your proof of payment until you receive a receipt.

Payment methods

Fees are paid by bank transfer or deposit into the school's official account. Cash can be paid at the school bursary during working hours against an official receipt. Please do not pay fees to individual staff members.

Payment deadline

Tuition for each term is due in full on or before resumption, and no later than the end of the second week of term unless an installment plan has been approved.

Installment option

Parents who cannot pay in full may ask the bursar in writing, before the term starts, to pay in installments. A typical plan is a first payment on or before resumption and the balance by mid-term. Every plan is confirmed in writing and must be completed before end-of-term examinations.

Late or failed payments

Fees not paid by the agreed date may attract a late-payment charge stated in the term's fee notice. Payments that are reversed or rejected by the bank count as unpaid. Persistent arrears may affect access to examinations or results, and parents are notified in writing first.

Sibling discounts

A concession on tuition may be available for the second and later children from the same family enrolled at the same time. Ask the bursar for the current rate; any discount is confirmed in writing and applies to tuition only.

Refunds & withdrawals

The registration / acceptance fee is non-refundable. Tuition paid for a term is non-refundable once the term has begun. To withdraw a child, give written notice of at least one term; otherwise a term's tuition may be charged in lieu of notice.

Receipts & records

Every payment receives an official receipt. Parents can also view and download invoices and receipts at any time in the School Portal.

Fee reviews

Fees are reviewed from time to time. Any change is communicated to parents in writing before the new session or term begins, and does not affect amounts already paid for the current term.

These terms apply to all pupils. For anything not covered here, please speak to the bursar.
