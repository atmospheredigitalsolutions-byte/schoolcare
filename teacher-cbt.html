How should my file look?

One question after another, numbered. For objective questions add options A to D and an answer line:

1. What is 2 + 3?
A. 4
B. 5
C. 6
D. 7
Answer: B
  • Options can be on separate lines or in one line (A. 4 B. 5 C. 6 D. 7).
  • No answer lines? Put an answer key at the end, for example ANSWERS: 1.B 2.C 3.A. You can also set any answer while reviewing.
  • Excel and CSV files use the columns Question, A, B, C, D, Answer, Marks (use the template above).
  • Scanned pages or photos can't be read. Use a typed file, or paste the text below.
  • Theory questions need no options. Write the question, numbered, and put the marks at the end like (5 marks). A line starting Answer: or Marking guide: after a question becomes its marking guide.
  • You can mix both kinds. A heading such as SECTION B: THEORY makes everything after it theory.
  • Objective questions only support four options (A to D).
