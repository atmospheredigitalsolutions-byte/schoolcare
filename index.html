What guides us

Al-Haqq Nur & Pry School is the institution where smartness, discipline, and diligence converge.

Vision

Empowering young minds with knowledge, skills and character to become compassionate and responsible leaders in a rapidly changing world.

Mission

To provide a nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence, spiritual growth, and promotes moral integrity.

Four simple steps to enrolling your child

From application to confirmation, admissions is handled carefully and communicated clearly at every stage.

01

Submit Application

Complete our enrollment form online with your child's bio-data and your contact details.

02

Document Review

Our admissions team reviews the application and verifies the information submitted.

03

Meet & Assess

A short, friendly interaction with your child and a meeting with parents or guardians.

04

Confirmation

Receive your admission decision and complete registration to secure your child's place.
