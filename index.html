What guides us

Al-Haqq Nur & Pry School is the institution where smartness, discipline, and diligence converge.

Vision

Empowering young minds with knowledge, skills and character to become compassionate and responsible leaders in a rapidly changing world.

Mission

To provide a nurturing environment that fosters academic excellence, spiritual growth, and promotes moral integrity.

Two stages, one foundation

Every child moves through a settled, faith-rooted path from their earliest years through primary school.

Early Years

Crèche and Nursery classes build early literacy, numeracy and Islamic values through guided play and observation.

  • Qaida and early Qur'an recitation
  • Phonics, numeracy and motor skills
  • Daily developmental observation records

Primary School

Primary 1 through 6 follow the National curriculum alongside structured Islamic studies and Arabic.

  • Continuous assessment and termly examinations
  • Qur'an, Hadith, Arabic and Islamic studies
  • Digital report cards shared with parents each term

Why families choose Al-Haqq

Every part of school life — attendance, results, safeguarding, fees — is tracked carefully and shared with parents in one place.

  • Qualified teachers across both Islamic and National curricula
  • Small class sizes with individual attention
  • Safeguarding and wellbeing records with controlled access
  • Parents can view results, attendance and fees online
